# SecureAI Terms of Service

**Status:** Draft for review. This document is a product-language starting point, not legal advice. Obtain review by qualified counsel in the jurisdictions where SecureAI is offered before publishing or relying on these terms.

## 1. Service description

SecureAI provides automated static analysis and AI-assisted security signals for source code, configuration, and AI/agent workflows. Depending on the enabled features, the service may identify possible vulnerabilities, risky capabilities, exposed credentials, prompt-injection paths, unsafe tool use, and related configuration concerns. Detection coverage varies by language, framework, repository structure, and scan configuration.

## 2. Findings are signals, not guarantees

Scan results, severity labels, confidence values, scores, explanations, and suggested remediations are estimates generated by rules, parsers, heuristics, and/or machine-learning systems. They may be incomplete, outdated, misleading, or incorrect. A finding is not proof that a vulnerability is exploitable; the absence of a finding is not proof that code is safe. Static analysis may miss runtime behavior, framework-specific controls, indirect data flows, dynamically generated code, dependencies, deployment settings, and vulnerabilities outside the scanned scope. False positives and false negatives are possible.

Do not use a SecureAI score as the sole basis for a security, compliance, release, procurement, or incident-response decision. Validate material findings in context and use qualified security review and appropriate testing for consequential decisions.

## 3. Severity and confidence

Severity represents an estimate of potential impact under the assumptions available to the scanner; it is not a guarantee of exploitability or likelihood. Confidence reflects the evidence available to a particular detection and may differ across detectors. A rule match, keyword, or nearby code pattern may be reported as a lead even when end-to-end data flow cannot be established. Review the finding's evidence, source location, scan coverage, and limitations before taking action.

## 4. Authorized use and repository rights

You must have the legal right and authorization to submit, access, and scan every repository, file, secret, and other material provided to SecureAI. Do not use the service to access or scan another person's or organization's code without permission. You are responsible for complying with applicable law, third-party licenses, organizational policies, and any obligations owed to repository owners.

## 5. Customer review and remediation

You are responsible for reviewing scan results and generated fixes before merging, deploying, or applying them. Suggested fixes may introduce defects, break functionality, weaken controls, or fail to address the underlying issue. Test changes in an appropriate environment, maintain backups where appropriate, and follow your normal review and release procedures. Rotate any credential that may have been exposed; deleting it from the current source alone may not revoke a previously compromised credential.

## 6. Sensitive information and submitted code

Do not submit credentials, personal data, regulated information, or confidential material unless you are authorized to do so and the submission is appropriate for your use of the service. Use supported configuration and secret-management mechanisms rather than embedding live credentials in source code. Review SecureAI's applicable Privacy Policy and product settings for details about processing, retention, access, and deletion. Do not assume that a scan is local-only or that submitted material is never transmitted to a service provider unless the product documentation for your specific deployment expressly says so.

## 7. Availability and coverage

Scans may be limited by file-selection rules, supported languages, parser failures, repository size, time budgets, configuration, or service availability. A scan report should identify available coverage information where supported. Files not analyzed, skipped files, parse failures, and unsupported languages can leave material risks undiscovered. You are responsible for checking scan coverage before relying on a report.

## 8. No professional or compliance certification

SecureAI does not, solely by producing a scan or score, provide a penetration test, formal audit, legal opinion, compliance certification, or guarantee of compliance with any standard or law. Any mappings to security frameworks are informational unless an expressly documented, independently validated assessment states otherwise.

## 9. Changes and support

Features, detection rules, supported languages, and scoring methods may change as the service is improved. Unless separately agreed in writing, SecureAI does not guarantee that a specific issue will be detected, that a result will be available continuously, or that every finding will include a complete remediation.
