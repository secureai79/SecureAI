Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{DATE}}

This policy explains what {{COMPANY}} ("we") collects when you use SecureAI, why, who else handles it, and your choices. Questions: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself), your workspace name and your role.
  • Team: the email addresses of people you invite, and who invited them.
  • Repositories and scans: repository names and URLs you add, and the results of scans: findings, risk scores and short explanatory messages.
  • Security and abuse logs: sign-in and account-security events with the time and IP address, used to protect accounts and apply rate limits.
  • Support: the messages you send us through the help form or by email.

2. Your source code

To scan a repository we copy it into a temporary workspace, analyse it statically (we do not run your code) and delete the workspace when the scan ends. By default we do not store raw source snippets: only structured findings are kept. Optional AI-based refinement of findings is off unless the operator of the service switches it on and records a privacy acknowledgement; in that case only short, redacted excerpts of flagged code are eligible.

When you connect GitHub we use a GitHub App with repository-scoped, read-only access. Short-lived access tokens are fetched when needed. We never ask for your GitHub password or one-time codes.

3. How long we keep it

  • Scan results are deleted automatically after 30 days (unless the service is configured otherwise).
  • Account and workspace data is kept until you ask us to delete it or the workspace is deleted.
  • Security logs are kept for a limited period needed to investigate abuse and incidents.
  • Encrypted database backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule and can take up to {{BACKUP_RETENTION}} to expire after a deletion.

4. Who else handles your data

We use these service providers (sub-processors) to run SecureAI. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting and delivery.
  • {{BACKEND_HOST}}: application hosting ({{HOSTING_REGION}}).
  • Neon: managed PostgreSQL database.
  • Brevo: sending transactional email (account confirmation, password reset, invitations, support).
  • GitHub: repository access you authorise.
  • Sentry (if enabled): error reports with credentials, request bodies and personal data removed.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

5. Security

Connections use TLS. Passwords are hashed, stored secrets such as GitHub tokens are encrypted, two-factor authentication is available, and sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you we will tell you without undue delay.

6. Cookies and local storage

SecureAI sets no cookies. Your browser stores your sign-in session and display preferences (theme, layout) locally so the app works; clearing it signs you out.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to or restrict certain processing. Workspace owners can delete scan data inside the product; for anything else write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. International transfers and children

Data is hosted in {{HOSTING_REGION}}; our providers may process it in other countries under appropriate safeguards. SecureAI is for business use and not for anyone under 16.

9. Changes and contact

We will post changes here and, for material changes, tell workspace owners by email. Contact: {{COMPANY}}, {{ADDRESS}}, {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
